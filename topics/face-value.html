Integrity rules this page inherits

  1. Any page that questions a claim shows the strongest case for it, at equal weight.

  3. No count appears without its denominator.
  4. Published is published — fading, drafting and commenting are not defences.
  5. The curator is recorded: who asked, when, and on what basis.
