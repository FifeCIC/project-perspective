Why this format

Perspectives is designed to provoke thought without sacrificing trust. A visitor should see the opposing claims quickly, be able to check the quality of the evidence behind them, and leave with a concrete next step rather than an opinion they were handed.

The unit of the site is not an article. It is a comparison: the same issue, the strongest version of each position, and a visible trail back to the source for every number used.

Evidence method

Tier 1

Official datasets, legislative records, peer-reviewed studies. Usable as the spine of a claim.

Tier 2

Reputable media and transparent policy research organisations. Corroborates, does not carry alone.

Tier 3

Expert commentary and public statements. Always carried with a confidence note and a date.

Rules that apply on every page: claims are paraphrased and attributed; the strongest counter-case is presented with equal weight and cannot be hidden behind a toggle; a figure without a date is treated as unverified.
